# Style guide

How the rule documents and every document of this repository are written.

## Language
- English. Names, titles, and quotes keep their original language.
- Present tense for what is true. Past tense for events and dated facts.
- Plain words and short sentences, one idea per sentence.

## Punctuation
- The only dash is the hyphen `-`. Em dashes and en dashes never appear.
- Semicolons never appear. Split the sentence.
- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Phrasing
- State facts directly. Contrast framings never appear: `X, not Y`, `rather than`, `instead of`, `not just X but Y`, `not only`.
- No hedging and no filler: `importantly`, `essentially`, `notably`, `it's worth noting`, `in summary`, `in conclusion`, `key takeaways`, `delve`.
- No relative time: `currently`, `recently`, `soon`, `today`. A fact that depends on time carries its date.

## Formatting
- No emoji. No bold or italics for emphasis.
- Headings name sections. A list holds items of the same kind.
- Links are relative markdown links, such as `[CR-13](cr-13-nesting-depth.md)`. External sources link to their URL.
